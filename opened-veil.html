The theme

Torn fromtop to bottom

When Jesus died, the curtain that closed off the holiest place in the temple was torn in two. Not from the bottom, where a man could reach, but from the top.

“And, behold, the veil of the temple was rent in twain from the top to the bottom.”

Matthew 27:51

The way that was shut is now open. Hebrews calls it a new and living way, through the veil, that is to say, his flesh. And Paul says the same veil that once lay over the reading of Scripture is taken away in Christ.

“Until this day remaineth the same vail untaken away in the reading of the old testament; which vail is done away in Christ.”

2 Corinthians 3:14

Opened Veil is our theme for 2027: a year of reading the Bible with the veil lifted, seeing how it holds together, why it can be trusted, and how every part of it points to him.
