Why this matters

Most believers are not short on faith. They are short on language.

Apologetics Nigeria exists to fix that — examining the claims of Islam through its own primary sources, making the historical case for Christ, and equipping ordinary believers to answer the questions they are actually being asked.

In October we run our first training. Over two hundred people have registered in a matter of weeks, from Lagos to Abuja to London to Houston. The demand is real, and it is well past what one person can carry.

So we are opening the doors. Not for busywork — for real responsibility, on work that reaches people who have nobody else answering their questions.

Where we need you

The roles.

Pick what you are genuinely good at. You can choose more than one, but choose honestly — we would rather have one thing done excellently.

Creative

Visual designer

Social graphics, flyers, presentation decks and campaign assets, working inside an established brand system. You should be comfortable in Figma, Illustrator or Photoshop, and comfortable being given a brand and asked to stay inside it.

Portfolio required
Creative

Video editor

Cutting teaching sessions into shorts and full episodes, subtitles, thumbnails, and turning three hours of recording into things people will actually watch. Premiere, Final Cut, DaVinci or CapCut — we care about the result, not the tool.

Showreel or samples required
Content

Content & social media manager

Owning the posting schedule across Instagram, TikTok and YouTube, writing captions, replying to comments and DMs, and reporting what is working. This role needs someone consistent rather than someone occasionally brilliant.

Accounts you have run
Content

Content writer

Scripts, articles, teaching notes and research summaries. Everything we publish is sourced — you will be asked to cite where a claim comes from, and to write carefully about people we disagree with.

Writing samples required
Operations

Notes & resources

Taking notes during sessions and turning them into the one-page summaries and slide packs we send out afterwards. If you are the person whose notes everyone borrows, this is your role.

Ongoing, session-based

Before you apply

Let us be straight with you.

This is unpaidWe are not in a position to pay yet. We will not pretend otherwise, and we will not waste your time.
It is remoteEverything runs online. Where you live does not matter — several of our people are outside Nigeria.
We need expertsNot enthusiasm alone. Send your portfolio, showreel or writing samples. We will actually look at them.
Consistency over volumeA few reliable hours a week beats a burst of brilliance followed by silence.
You get the creditYour name on the work, the pieces for your portfolio, and a reference whenever you need one.
You will be stretchedReal briefs, real deadlines, real audiences. You will get better at your craft here.
